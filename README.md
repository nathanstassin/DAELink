## Overview
For detailed installation and usage instructions, visit the [project page](https://nathanstassin.com/daelink).

DAELink bridges the gap between Adobe After Effects and Blackmagic DaVinci Resolve.

### Key Features

- **Composition Linking:** Bidirectional links between AE comps and DaVinci timeline nests. Jump straight from a DaVinci timeline clip to the exact linked composition in After Effects.
- **Marker Synchronisation:** Share notes, animation timings, and comments between apps.
- **Smart Render Management:** Render in one click from After Effects with templates, then refresh renders in DaVinci in one click.
- **Layer Transfer:** Send video, audio, and image layers from DaVinci timelines to your AE project in a new comp.
- **Project Organisation:** Each project maintains its own `daelink` folder, auto-organised in apps and on disk.

**All files are local on your computer. No cloud, no signups, no hidden fees.**
---

## Requirements

- **DaVinci Resolve:** Studio v20+ or Free 19.0.3
- **After Effects:** CC 2025 or later
- **Operating System:** macOS, Windows
